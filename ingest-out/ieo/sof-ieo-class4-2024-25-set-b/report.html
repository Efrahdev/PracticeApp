Curiously, he stopped to observe them, amused by their laughter and camaraderie. Turning to Birbal, Akbar remarked, “Look at these children, Birbal. They are so carefree and happy. Intrigued by Birbal’s response, Akbar issued a challenge. “Birbal, I want you to bring me something that will make me both laugh and cry.” Birbal accepted the challenge with his characteristic confidence and set out to fulfil the emperor’s request. Days passed, and Akbar grew impatient, wondering if Birbal would succeed.

Finally, one evening, Birbal returned to the palace with a small package wrapped in colourful cloth. Presenting it to Akbar, he said, “Your Majesty, I have found something that I believe will meet your challenge.”

Birbal smiled and said, “Your Majesty, when you look into this mirror, you will see yourself. And in that reflection, you will find both laughter and tears. Laughter for the joyous moments you have experienced and tears for the hardships you have overcome.”

Impressed by Birbal’s wisdom, Akbar laughed heartily, realizing the profound truth in his words. From that day onward, he cherished the mirror as a reminder of the dualities of life and the wisdom of his beloved advisor, Birbal.

Laughter is indeed a wonderful medicine that can heal our hearts and minds. Just like how a bandage can mend a cut or a pill can cure an ailment, laughter has the magical power to uplift our spirits and make us feel better, both mentally and physically.

Imagine a world without laughter; it would be dull and dreary, wouldn’t it? But fortunately, we have the gift of laughter, which adds colour and joy to our lives. Whether it’s a hearty chuckle, a giggle, or a belly laugh, the sound of laughter is like music to our ears. It has the incredible ability to lighten even the heaviest of burdens and turn our frowns upside down.

When we laugh, our bodies release endorphins, which are natural chemicals that promote feelings of happiness and well-being. So, not only does laughter make us feel good emotionally, but it also has positive effects on our physical health. It can reduce stress, boost our immune system, and even relieve pain. That’s why they say, “Laughter is the best medicine.”

Moreover, laughter is contagious! Have you ever noticed how when one person starts laughing, others can’t help but join in? It’s like a ripple effect, spreading joy and happiness wherever it goes. Whether it’s sharing a funny joke with friends, watching a comedy show, or simply finding humor in everyday situations, laughter brings people together and strengthens bonds.

In addition to its health benefits, laughter enhances our creativity and problem- solving abilities. When we’re in a relaxed and jovial mood, our minds become more open and receptive to new ideas. So, the next time you’re stuck on a difficult task, take a moment to laugh—you might just find the solution you’ve been looking for! In conclusion, laughter truly is a marvellous medicine that enriches our lives in countless ways. It’s a powerful tool for promoting happiness, fostering connections, and improving our overall well-being. So, let’s make laughter a regular part of our lives and spread its healing effects far and wide!

A

happiness

B

well-being

C

stress

D

Both A and B

Answer D
