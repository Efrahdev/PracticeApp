Q20 · Mathematics · p68

40 students and 2 teachers folded some paper cranes. The teachers folded twice as fast as the students. If each student folded 4 paper cranes, how many paper cranes did they fold altogether?

A

168

B

176

C

192

D

224

Answer B

4 × 2 = 8

2 × 8 = 16

No. of students = 40

Each students folds 4 paper cranes = 40 × 4 = 160

No. of teachers = 2

Each teacher folds paper cranes twice of a student = 4 × 2 = 8 → 2 × 8 = 16

Total paper = 160 + 16 = 176 cranes.
